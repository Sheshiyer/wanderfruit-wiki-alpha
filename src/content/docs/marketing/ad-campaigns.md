## PHASE 1: Strategic Calibration

### Step 1: Goal Analysis
**Campaign Goal:** Balanced Awareness + Conversion  
**Strategic Weight:** 60% brand awareness (emotional hooks, storytelling, curiosity) / 40% conversion (direct offers, urgency, feature stacking)

**Rationale:**  
WanderFruit is entering market against established competitors (Airbnb, Hotels). Need to build brand recognition AND drive immediate bookings. Balanced approach educates market on "sweet spot" positioning while capturing ready-to-book travelers.

### Step 2: Persona Injection
**Bleeding Neck Pain Point:**  
*"I spent three hours comparing Airbnb listings and still ended up in a place where the WiFi barely worked, the photos were from 2019, and the 'responsive host' took two days to answer. I'm exhausted from being a detective just to book a vacation rental."*

**Dream State:**  
*"I book in minutes with complete confidence, arrive to find everything exactly as promised, feel part of the neighborhood instead of isolated, and actually enjoy my trip instead of stressing about accommodation logistics."*

### Step 3: Product Extraction
**Hero Feature:** Pre-Curated Quality Control (every property verified, photos accurate, amenities tested)  
**Killer Spec:** Verified 500Mbps+ WiFi tested for video calls + <2hr host response time guaranteed

### Step 4: Tone Setting
**Ad Voice:** "Confident Friend" (balanced awareness/conversion)  
- Conversational enough to build rapport
- Confident enough to drive action
- Warm enough to feel approachable
- Direct enough to convey urgency

## PHASE 2: Campaign Concept (The Big Idea)

### Step 5: Campaign Name
**Internal Code:** "Operation: Sweet Spot"  
**Theme:** Finding your perfect intersection of location + comfort + authenticity

### Step 6: Hook Strategy
**Why This Angle Works:**

Explorer Emma is drowning in Airbnb decision fatigue. She's opened 47 tabs, read 200 reviews, cross-referenced Google Maps street views, and she STILL doesn't trust her choice. The hook that stops her scroll is the promise of *"someone already did this work for you."*

The "sweet spot" metaphor does heavy lifting:
1. **Tangible visual:** Easy to understand (intersection point on Venn diagram)
2. **Aspirational:** Everyone wants to find their sweet spot
3. **Relief-based:** Implies the hard work is done
4. **Memorable:** Sticky brand concept

We lead with the pain (Airbnb anxiety) and pivot immediately to the solution (we've curated the good places). The scroll-stop moment is recognition: *"Oh my god, someone gets it. I'm exhausted too."*

### Step 7: Visual Directive
**Master Creative Style:**

**Primary Visual Concept:** Split-screen storytelling
- LEFT: Airbnb anxiety (person stressed at laptop, 47 open tabs, confused expression)
- RIGHT: WanderFruit ease (person relaxed on Bangkok balcony, coffee in hand, confident smile)
- CENTER: "Find your sweet spot" text overlay

**Secondary Visual Concepts:**
- Bangkok rooftop pool at sunset (aspirational lifestyle)
- Clean modern apartment interior matching professional photos (trust signal)
- Digital nomad working from verified workspace (use case proof)
- Local Bangkok neighborhood street scene (authentic living promise)

**Video Style:** 15-30 sec with quick cuts
- Open: Frustrated Airbnb browsing
- Transition: "There's a better way"
- Showcase: Beautiful properties, happy guests, neighborhood vibes
- Close: "Find your sweet spot at WanderFruit.com"

## CAMPAIGN PERFORMANCE TRACKING

### Primary Metrics by Ad Type

**Awareness Headlines (H2, H4, H6):**
- Target CTR: 2-4%
- Target Engagement Rate: 5-8%
- Success = Brand recall, website traffic

**Conversion Headlines (H1, H3, H5):**
- Target CTR: 1.5-3%
- Target Conversion Rate: 3-6%
- Success = Property views, bookings

**Body Copy Performance:**
- Feature Lists (B1): Best for consideration stage
- Benefits (B2): Best for cold traffic awareness
- Testimonials (B5): Best for trust building
- Problem Agitation (B4): Best for retargeting

### A/B Testing Roadmap

**Week 1-2:** Test headline types (which gets highest CTR?)
- Run H1.1, H2.1, H4.1, H5.1 simultaneously
- 25% budget each
- Winner gets 50% budget Week 3

**Week 3-4:** Test body copy formats
- Run B1.1, B2.1, B4.1, B5.1
- Pair with winning headline
- Measure engagement depth + conversion

**Week 5-6:** Test creative formats
- Static image vs carousel vs video
- Instagram Feed vs Stories vs Reels
- YouTube 15s vs 30s

**Week 7-8:** Optimization
- Scale winners
- Kill losers
- Refine targeting based on conversion data
