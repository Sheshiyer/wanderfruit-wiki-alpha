### FAQ Matrix (Top 10 Questions)

**Q1: How is WanderFruit different from Airbnb?**  
**A:** Airbnb is an open marketplace - anyone can list anything without verification. We curate. Every property is professionally managed, WiFi is tested, photos are accurate, BTS proximity is verified. We solve the trust problem Airbnb created.

**Q2: Are these more expensive than Airbnb?**  
**A:** Our Bangkok 1BR condos average 1,500-3,000 THB/night - similar to Airbnb's 1,815 THB average. But you're not gambling on whether it works. Better value = no disaster risk + transparent pricing.

**Q3: How do I know the WiFi actually works?**  
**A:** Every property is speed tested before listing (minimum 500Mbps). We have screenshots on file. Many listings show the speed test results. Remote workers can book with confidence.

**Q4: What if something breaks during my stay?**  
**A:** Professional management = <2 hour response time. AC breaks? Maintenance same day. WiFi issues? IT support dispatched. You have a phone number that actually gets answered. 24/7 emergency hotline for urgent issues.

**Q5: Can I see the property before booking?**  
**A:** Photos are professional and accurate (updated every 6 months). For longer stays (30+ days), video calls with property managers can be arranged. We guarantee photo accuracy - if reality doesn't match, we'll relocate you immediately.

**Q6: Are these properties legal to operate?**  
**A:** We partner with professionally managed properties handling compliance. Co.Property manages building relationships and insurance. Unlike the 16,806 unlicensed Airbnb listings in Bangkok, we prioritize proper operations.

**Q7: What's included in the price?**  
**A:** Transparent pricing. Cleaning fees clearly stated. Electricity INCLUDED in monthly rates (unlike many Airbnbs that surprise you with bills). No hidden charges. What you see is what you pay.

**Q8: How far is BTS/MRT really?**  
**A:** We walk every route ourselves and time it. "5 min walk" means verified 5 minutes, not 20. Listings show exact walk time + Google Maps links. No location exaggeration.

**Q9: Can I book for just a weekend?**  
**A:** Minimum stays vary by property (most: 3-4 nights minimum). Some properties offer weekend bookings. Use filters to find properties matching your stay length.

**Q10: What if I need to cancel?**  
**A:** Cancellation policies vary by property (flexible, moderate, strict). Clearly stated before booking. Most hosts offer flexible cancellation up to 7 days before check-in. Review policy during booking process.
