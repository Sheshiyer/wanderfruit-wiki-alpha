### 2. **Competitor Intelligence Dossier** (`competitor-intelligence-airbnb.md`)
**Purpose:** Systematic teardown of Airbnb's weaknesses  
**Key Insight:** 8 major market gaps WanderFruit can exploit

**What's Inside:**
- Hard data (Bangkok: 15,232 listings, THB 1,815 avg price, 0 licensed properties)
- Specific complaints from real reviews (WiFi failures, bait-and-switch photos, unresponsive hosts)
- Customer anxiety matrix (what travelers worry about before booking)
- Strategic implications (how WanderFruit wins)

**Market Gaps Identified:**
1. No quality control or amenity verification
2. Inconsistent host communication
3. WiFi reliability crisis (remote workers can't verify speeds)
4. Photo accuracy crisis (AI-generated images, old photos)
5. Review system gaming (fake reviews, rating pressure)
6. Location misrepresentation (BTS distances exaggerated)
7. Legal grey zone (zero licensed Bangkok properties)
8. Amateur host problems (unprofessional communication)

**How to Use:**
- Competitive positioning messaging (we solve X that Airbnb fails at)
- Landing page objection handling
- Sales conversations ("How are you different from Airbnb?")
- Product roadmap (prioritize features that address gaps)

### 5. **Email Sequences** (`email-sequences-wanderfruit.md`)
**Purpose:** Complete email automation flows for customer lifecycle  
**Key Insight:** 3 sequences covering signup → booking → post-stay

**What's Inside:**

**Sequence 1: Newsletter Welcome**
- Email 1A: Immediate welcome + browse CTAs
- Email 1B: Day 3 neighborhood guide (if no booking)

**Sequence 2: Booking Confirmation & Pre-Arrival**
- Email 2A: Immediate booking confirmation (reduce anxiety)
- Email 2B: 48hrs pre-arrival (check-in instructions, neighborhood tips)
- Email 2C: 24hrs post-checkout (review request, return discount)

**Sequence 3: Abandoned Browse Recovery**
- Email 3A: 24hrs after 3+ property views (comparison table, decision help)

**Automation Rules:**
- Trigger conditions
- Timing specifications
- Suppression logic
- Personalization variables

**How to Use:**
- Email platform setup (Mailchimp, Klaviyo, Customer.io)
- Copy-paste subject lines and body copy
- Customize variables ({{PROPERTY_NAME}}, {{HOST_NAME}}, etc.)
- A/B test subject line variants
- Monitor open rates and adjust
